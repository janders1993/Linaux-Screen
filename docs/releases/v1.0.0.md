The first release: turn your phone or tablet into a second monitor for your KDE Plasma PC, wirelessly, through the browser.

## What's in it

- **A real second screen:** a virtual monitor in KDE's Display settings, shown in any modern browser.
- **Several devices at once:** up to four devices watch the same screen.
- **Sound to a device:** pick which device plays the PC's sound from the tray. The PC is always the fallback.
- **Touch, trackpad and keyboard control:** tap, hold, drag and scroll, plus Bluetooth keyboards and the iPad's trackpad. Each device must ask, and you Accept or Deny on the PC.
- **Quality presets per device:** Battery saver, Smooth, Sharp and Ultra, switched instantly.
- **Tray icon and auto-stop:** starts only when you open it, and stops a minute after the last device leaves.

## Install

Download `linaux-screen-1.0.0-x86_64.tar.gz` below, then:

```bash
tar xf linaux-screen-1.0.0-x86_64.tar.gz
cd linaux-screen-1.0.0-x86_64
./install.sh
```

Then open **Linaux Screen** from the app menu. The [README](https://github.com/janders1993/Linaux-Screen#readme) explains the rest.

**Needs:** KDE Plasma 6 on Wayland. Built on Fedora 44, for Bazzite and other Fedora 44–based systems; on other distributions, [build from source](https://github.com/janders1993/Linaux-Screen/blob/main/docs/DEVELOPMENT.md). The installer checks your PC for the streaming pieces it needs, and on Bazzite adds GStreamer's `nicesrc` to your home folder.

Tested on Bazzite with an NVIDIA GPU, a Samsung Galaxy phone and an iPad.
